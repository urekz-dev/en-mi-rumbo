# ADR 0011: Photos in R2, transformed on upload with Cloudflare Images

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-11
- Owner: Alexis Zazueta

## Context
The prototype resized photos with sharp, which does not run on Workers, and stored them in a private Supabase bucket served through a route of its own. Cloudflare Images offers a Workers binding with 5,000 unique transformations per month free; reading image metadata is free. R2 buckets can be exposed on a custom hostname cached by the CDN with no Worker invocation.

## Decision
The registration form uploads the photo to the Worker, which validates type and size, transforms it once with the Images binding into two WebP sizes (card and full) with metadata stripped, and stores only those in R2. Originals are not kept. Each environment has its own bucket and hostname: `fotos.enmirumbo.com`, `fotos-stage.enmirumbo.com`, `fotos-dev.enmirumbo.com`. Keys are opaque and generated by the server; a photo of an unpublished business is not referenced anywhere public.

## Consequences
Two transformations per photo, so the free allowance covers far more uploads than expected. Listing pages do not spend Worker requests on images. Photos of businesses that are deleted are removed before the row, and an orphan sweep runs in the scheduled job.
